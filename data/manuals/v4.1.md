# Nexus Sight Edge Platform — Manual v4.1

Applies to camera firmware 4.1 (NX-Dome 4K, NX-Fisheye 360) and the Edge Analytics gateway 4.x.

## 1. What changed from v3
- On-device people counting: the camera sends counts instead of video for the people-counting module, reducing
  upstream bandwidth by ~70 %.
- Health score (0–100) combines heartbeat regularity, FPS stability, focus score and packet loss.
- Secure boot and signed firmware: a camera refuses firmware that is not signed by Nexus Sight.

## 2. Troubleshooting by health score
| Score | Interpretation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 85–100 | healthy | none |
| 40–84 | degraded | see issue code; open a ticket if it persists for 24 h |
| 0–39 | failing or offline | follow the offline checklist, open a high-priority ticket |

## 3. Offline checklist
1. Confirm the store has power and internet (other cameras online?).
2. Check the PoE switch port; move the camera to a spare port.
3. Power-cycle the camera from the switch.
4. If still offline after 15 minutes, open a `camera_offline` ticket with the camera id.

## 4. Maintenance windows
- A window takes cameras offline for analytics; loss-prevention alerts pause.
- Windows longer than 2 hours or covering more than 10 cameras require approval from a second manager.
- Customers are notified with the `maintenance_notice` template at least 24 h in advance.
