# Nexus Sight Edge Platform — Manual v3

Applies to camera firmware 3.x (NX-Bullet 2) and the Edge Analytics gateway 3.x.

## 1. Network requirements
- Upstream bandwidth: 2 Mbit/s per 1080p stream, 6 Mbit/s per 4K stream.
- Ports: 443/TCP to the cloud ingest, 554/TCP (RTSP) inside the store network only.
- PoE budget: 12.95 W per camera (802.3af).

## 2. Health states
| State | Meaning | First action |
|---|---|---|
| online | heartbeat in the last 2 minutes, FPS ≥ 12 | none |
| degraded | heartbeat present but FPS < 12, blur or packet loss | check lens and uplink utilisation |
| offline | no heartbeat for 5 minutes | check PoE switch port, then power-cycle |

## 3. Issue codes
- `NO_HEARTBEAT` — camera unreachable. Verify the switch port LED and PoE budget.
- `POE_FAILURE` — the switch reports a power fault on the port. Replace the patch cable before the camera.
- `LOW_FPS` — encoder overloaded or uplink saturated. Lower the bitrate profile to "balanced".
- `IMAGE_BLUR` — focus drift or dirty lens. Clean the dome; re-run autofocus from the console.
- `PACKET_LOSS` — more than 2 % loss on RTSP. Check duplex settings and cable length (< 90 m).

## 4. Maintenance
Firmware upgrades take 6–10 minutes per camera and reboot the device twice. Schedule them between 01:00 and
05:00 local time. Loss-prevention alerts are not generated while a camera is in maintenance.
